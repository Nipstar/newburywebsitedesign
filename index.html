What Newbury firms ask before they ring

Thatcham and Hungerford: are they covered as well?

Yes, along with the rest of West Berkshire, Greenham, Speen, Kingsclere and Lambourn included. Calls and video do most of the work, and your postcode has no effect on the price.

Can you take over my existing site and domain?

Yes. Andy builds the replacement as a preview while the old one stays up, then points your domain across at launch once you are happy. The domain stays in your name throughout. You own the site once it is paid for.

Is a 5-page site enough?

For most sole traders, yes. A usual set is home, services, about, areas covered and contact, which gives each common question somewhere to live. A firm with several separate services will get more from the 8-page Core.

What should a Newbury business budget for a website?

Allow £600 for the 5-page Starter or £995 for the 8-page Core, and £125 for any page past that. Hosting and care then runs at £10 a month, so you can see the whole cost at the outset.

How soon could the site be answering customers?

Think 1 to 3 weeks once the deposit is paid. Having your service details and photos ready at the start pulls it towards the shorter end.

Who holds the keys when it is finished?

You own the site once it is paid for. Hosting and care is a service you pay for, not a lease on your own website.
